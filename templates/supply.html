A depot in Kalo Xorio, and a car for the rest of Cyprus.

Vittorio supplies coffee, chocolate, teas, syrups, mixes, and serviceware to cafés and bars. Orders are packed at Synergasias 17, Kalo Xorio, and delivered by car.

  • Delivery covers all of Cyprus.
  • Nothing is sent abroad.
  • The counter is open every day from 08:00 to 20:00.
  • Published prices are plus VAT. A line with no published price is confirmed before delivery.
